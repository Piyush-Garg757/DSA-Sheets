 Traversal techniques:
 1. DFS - Depth first search . It has further 3 types :
          1. Inorder Traversal (Left Root Right)
          2. Preorder Traversal (Root Left Right)
          3. Postorder Traversal (Left Right Root)

 2. BFS - Breadth first search - level by level print karega


 Traversals Given	Unique Tree Banega?
Inorder + Preorder	✅ Yes
Inorder + Postorder	✅ Yes
Preorder + Postorder	❌ No (generally)
Ab dekhte hain kyun.

Case 1: Inorder + Preorder
Suppose
Preorder : A B D E C F
Inorder  : D B E A C F
Step 1
Preorder ka pehla element hamesha root hota hai.
Root = A
Ab inorder me A ko dhoondo.
D B E |A| C F
Inorder automatically split ho gaya.
Left subtree
D B E
Right subtree
C F
Ab preorder me pata hai ki root ke baad pehle left subtree ke nodes aayenge.
To recursively same process.
Isliye tree uniquely ban jata hai.

Case 2: Inorder + Postorder
Example
Postorder : D E B F C A
Inorder   : D B E A C F
Postorder ka last element root hota hai.
Root = A
Inorder me A ko dhoondo.
D B E |A| C F
Fir left aur right subtree clearly separate ho gaye.
Again recursion.
Unique tree.
Sabse important baat
Inorder hi batata hai ki root ke left me kaun hai aur right me kaun hai.
Left Subtree | Root | Right Subtree
Ye information kisi aur traversal me nahi hoti.
